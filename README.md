# My Favorite Series 

## Series

### 1. *Twinkling Watermelon* (2023)

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Genre | Fantasy, Romance, Coming-of-age, Time travel |
| Seasons / Episodes | 1 season · 16 episodes |
| Created by | Son Jong-hyun |
| Where to watch | Viu |
| Status | Finished |
| My rating | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5) |
| Rewatch count | 3 |

**Why I love it:** No matter how many times I watch this series, I WOULD cry buckets. The storyline is different from other KDramas out there, and it is one of a kind. There is a mix of sadness, agony, comedy, and anger each episode. It really is a rollercoaster ride, but it was so much fun to watch!

**Favorite episode:** "The Final Festival" (S1,E13)— Honestly, all of the episodes are my favorite, but I'll just choose this one. This episode is where Han-Gyul's parents officially declared their romantic connection with each other through an emotional sign language piece by Yi-chan to Chung-Ah. I'm crying my eyes out just typing this.

**Favorite character:** *Ha Yi Chan* — Hyun Wook really portrayed Yi Chan's character very effectively. He is a loving father and husband despite being deaf. I like Han Gyul's character, too, because it shows how much a child could love his family to be able to sacrifice much just to connect them to the world.

**Favorite quote:**
> "I'll be born as your father in my next life"
